Momentum rarely arrives as a dramatic burst of inspiration. More often, it
looks like making the next decision small enough to begin.

Here are a few practices I return to when a project feels stuck.

## Lower the cost of starting

Leave a useful edge unfinished: a question at the bottom of a note, a component
with an obvious next state, or a sketch that needs one more pass. Beginning is
easier when the first move is already visible.

## Add a constraint

An open field can be surprisingly difficult to cross. A time limit, a fixed
format, or a deliberately small audience gives the work something to push
against.

## Keep evidence of progress

Save the rough drafts. Write down what changed. Small evidence makes progress
easier to see, especially before the work is polished enough to feel finished.

Momentum is not speed. It is a relationship with the work that makes returning
to it feel possible.
